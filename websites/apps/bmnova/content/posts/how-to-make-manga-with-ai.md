## Quick Answer

To make your own manga with AI, work like a mangaka, not like an image prompter: **write the scene first, define your cast once, lock a single style, then generate full pages and continue from the last one.** The hardest part is character consistency (the same face, hair and outfit in every panel), and it is solved by reusing a fixed character definition, ideally with a reference photo, instead of re-describing the hero in every prompt.

---

## What You Need Before You Start

- **A scene:** three to six story beats, such as "she finds the letter, hesitates, runs to the station"
- **A protagonist:** name, role, age range, one or two visual anchors (red scarf, scar over the eye)
- **A format:** manga (right-to-left, black and white feel), manhwa (vertical, colour) or Western comic
- **A genre:** action, romance, fantasy, isekai and so on, because it shapes pacing and framing

---

## How to Make a Manga Page with AI, Step by Step

1. **Script the beats.** One line per panel is enough. Put the dialogue in quotes so it ends up in speech bubbles.
2. **Build the cast.** Add a reference photo and a short description for each recurring character. Keep the description identical from page to page.
3. **Pick the visual DNA.** Choose format, art style (shonen, seinen, shojo, cyberpunk) and genre once for the whole chapter.
4. **Generate the full page.** Ask for a multi-panel layout, not a single illustration, so panel flow and pacing come out together.
5. **Fix, don't restart.** Edit a bubble or regenerate the one panel that is off; keep the rest.
6. **Continue from the last page.** Write the next beats and generate page two with the same cast and style.

---

## Why AI Characters Change Between Panels

General image generators treat every prompt as a new picture. They have no memory of the face they drew a minute ago, so small wording changes give you a new hairstyle, a new jawline or a different age.

| Approach | Consistency | Effort |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Re-describe the hero in every prompt | Low | High |
| Same text description, copy-pasted | Medium | Medium |
| Reference photo + saved character | High | Low |

## FAQ

**Can I make a manga of myself with AI?**
Yes. Use a clear, front-facing photo as the character reference and keep the same description for every page.

**What is the difference between manga, manhwa and comics?**
Manga is Japanese and traditionally read right to left in black and white. Manhwa is Korean, often full colour and vertical for phones, and Western comics usually read left to right in colour.

**How do I keep the same character across AI comic pages?**
Define the character once, reuse the exact same reference and description, and continue from the previous page instead of starting a new generation from scratch.

**Do I need to know how to draw to make AI manga?**
No. You need a story and clear choices about cast and style; the AI handles the drawing, and you edit dialogue and panels.
